- **Horizontal mechanics checked:** the needle has a tip slot. R/L changes it one slot. Horizontally threaded beads attempt to translate with it, but strand pins, bead collisions, the left anchor, and vertical-strand barriers can block individual contiguous segments. Retracting past a threaded bead sheds it; an empty gap costs an action. Advancing into a bead may pierce or push it depending on whether it can move; a right-wall-blocked bead is pierced. Gaps in a threaded chain persist through motion.
- **Vertical mechanics checked:** U/D carries every H-threaded bead. The entire active needle span through its tip can push any encountered loose/vertical-only bead one row, even where no H bead occupies that column. Beads enter vertical destination strands only by vertical motion; lateral shifting onto a strand is blocked. Leaving a strand end removes vertical credit; retracting the horizontal needle from a cross-threaded bead deposits it vertically.
- **Level 4 solved/verified algorithm:** push second beads down/out with the bare span; align first beads below strand ends and carry them vertically in; deposit firsts; route an empty short needle below the seconds and push them upward beneath the firsts. This established bare-needle pushing in both directions and that target strand order, not a fixed top row, matters.
