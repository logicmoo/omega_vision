## Working model

A magenta anchor rides the fixed vertical rail just left of the room and starts with one patterned lead slot pointing right.
- **Checked controls:** ACTION1/ACTION2 translate the entire current assembly up/down one logical row (6 px). This works even while the cable and a multi-block chain are extended (step 19). ACTION4 extends the active terminal right one slot: with no acquired block it lengthens cable; with blocks it pushes the whole acquired chain right while magenta stays fixed. ACTION3 retracts/pulls the whole acquired chain left one slot. ACTION7 is undo; ACTION6 remains unused/likely alternate mouse input.
- **Checked acquisition/objective:** The bottom panel gives the required acquisition order from magenta left-to-right. When the terminal's outgoing right coupling reaches the next required 4x4 colored target, ACTION4 attaches it on the right and hollows that target's 2x2 center in the bottom panel. Acquired blocks preserve order and move rigidly. Contacting the final required target immediately completes the level; no final retraction is needed (step 20 raised levels 0->1).
- **Checked movement details:** A successful ordinary horizontal move shifts the active colored chain by 6 px and lays/removes cable; contact instead leaves the existing terminal in place and attaches the target one slot to its right. Vertical motion translates magenta, cable, and all acquired blocks together. Empty lead/colored-chain movement leaves no persistent trail.
- **Checked HUD:** y=53 is a full-width budget/timer strip, not gameplay. In level 1 it advanced one cell every three actions after casting began; the budget was very loose and resets on the next level. The alternating thin vertical strip beside magenta is its fixed rail, not a timer.
- **Unresolved/new-level mechanic:** Whether cable or acquired blocks may pass under/over unacquired targets has not yet been observed. Do not assume collision behavior; probe it with a vertical move from a clear row rather than deliberately extending into a wrong-order target.
