# Working model
- **Checked:** Control is only `ACTION6(x,y)`. A tethered system has one rounded 5×5 player, N diamond endpoint blobs, and straight color-1 tethers. Its player coordinate is `floor(componentwise mean(endpoint centers))`; this has held for N=2, 3, and 4. Earlier levels used a color-6 player core; level 5 instead has white/colorable players with no special center core and color-4 endpoint centers.
- **Checked:** Exactly one endpoint globally is active, shown by a color-0 body. A normal field click relocates that active endpoint center exactly to the click, with no range limit, then recomputes its system. Clicking any body cell of an inactive color-3 endpoint only selects it (old active 0→3, selected 3→0). Thus changing endpoints normally costs select + destination.
- **Checked:** A player solves a matching dotted ring when it enters the ring interior; exact center is sufficient but not necessary. A solved ring flashes and settles with all 12 perimeter cells changed to 0; its assembly remains until the whole level completes.
- **Checked:** Players and rings can be multicolored, and directional color pattern matters. On level 4, each of three differently partitioned players solved only its uniquely identical ring; decoy rings stayed unchanged.
- **Checked:** Every corresponding player/ring on a multi-player level must be solved; a level advances after the last one.
- **Checked:** Color 2 is wall terrain. The x=0 strip is a fresh 64-action timer per level, filling one cell 0→5 per action; it is not gameplay.
- **Strongly supported by every route, collision itself unprobed:** Require every endpoint/player shape and every exact Bresenham tether to avoid color 2, large color-10 terrain when present, and other assemblies. Small isolated colored cells can be ring parts. `scratch/geom.py` exactly reproduces all observed tether cells.
- **Checked planning arithmetic:** A final endpoint-coordinate sum in `[N*T, N*T+N-1]` per axis puts the floored mean at exact target T. Obstacles may force waypoint moves or moving a currently inactive endpoint first.
- **Level-5 hypothesis, structurally strong but not yet live-tested:** White players acquire colored portions by exactly overlapping filled 5×5 half-color “paint stations,” carrying that directional pattern after leaving. Two complementary stations should combine on one player, which can then solve the identically patterned ring. Do not commit past the first carry-away test until confirmed.
