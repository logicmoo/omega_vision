- **Checked through level 4:** A level completes only when every 3x3 color-4 marker center is simultaneously covered by shape geometry of the marker center's required color. Same-color shapes may cover their markers collectively. Merely covering all centers in wrong colors does not work (level 4).
- **Checked controls:** ACTION1 up 3, ACTION2 down 3, ACTION3 left 3, ACTION4 right 3. ACTION5 cycles shapes in generation order, restores the old reference point's transported underlay, and blackens the new shape's reference point.
- **Checked movement/layers:** Shapes translate rigidly in 3-cell strides, may overlap markers/frames/other shapes, restore underlays when uncovered, and may clip at edges. Rendering uses persistent object z-order, so another shape can hide the selected shape's black anchor.
- **Checked anchor nuance:** Do not infer a moved anchor's restoration solely from its initial center pixel; retrodict the transported pattern. Paint-station contact repaints the selected reference underlay too, so later deselection can restore the painted color at its anchor.
- **Checked completion:** A successful final translation immediately loads the next level. Omit old-board expectations on that action and use `expect_levels`.
- **Checked paint stations (level 4):** Any selected-shape pixel touching any part of a framed 6x6 station, including its color-2 frame, repaints the whole selected shape to the 4x4 interior color; anchor entry is unnecessary. If a shape touches multiple stations, a later-processed station can win (right station beat middle station in level 4).
- **HUD:** Bottom row is a non-terrain action/deadline bar (base color15, fill often color1); do not treat it as gameplay.
