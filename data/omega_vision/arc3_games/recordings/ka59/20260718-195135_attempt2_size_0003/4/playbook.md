- **Checked through level 1:** Movable pieces are color-14 shells with a core. Core color 0 marks the selected piece. ACTION6 must click a piece's core (clicking its shell is inert); the clicked piece becomes selected/core 0 and the formerly selected piece becomes core 4. Initially unselected pieces can instead have core 5. Only the selected piece responds directly to arrows.
- **Checked through level 1:** Color-4 outlined frames are goals. Each frame's interior has exactly the dimensions of its matching piece. A level completes automatically once every piece occupies its matching frame; individual placements do not lock and pieces remain reselectable. Level 1 completed when both 3x3 pieces occupied the two 5x5 frames.
- **Checked through level 1:** A selected piece cannot cross a solid color-15 barrier alone. To transport an inactive piece through a barrier, put it at the floor edge, line the selected piece directly behind it, and press toward the barrier. The inactive piece animates through/across the barrier while the selected pusher stays on the source side. In level 1 a rightward push sent the inactive 3x3 piece from gate center (31,31) through centers 34,37,40,43 to (46,31).

Exact landing rules after transport (target alignment vs. shape/obstacle stopping) need observation in level 2; do not assume them beyond one tested transport.
